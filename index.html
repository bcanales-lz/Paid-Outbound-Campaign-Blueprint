Connect Google Sheet

Paste the published CSV URL from the Wpromote Google Sheet. The dashboard will fetch live data every time it opens — no manual edits needed when campaigns change.

How to get the published CSV URL

  1. Open the Google Sheet in your browser
  2. Go to File → Share → Publish to web
  3. Select the "Campaign Data" sheet and CSV format
  4. Click Publish and copy the URL
  5. Paste above and click Save & Reload
